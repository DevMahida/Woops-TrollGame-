## Data & Event Flow

1. **Player Spawn & Checkpoint Setup**:
   - `ATrollGameCharacter::BeginPlay()` initializes base movement values, scans for `Start` actor, `Checkpoint` actors, `RoomScreenActor`, and `PressurePlate` actors.
2. **Pressure Plate Trap Execution**:
   - `ATrollGameCharacter::Tick()` detects proximity to `ATrollPressurePlate`.
   - `ATrollPressurePlate::IsPlayerOnTrap()` calculates relative 2D distance to determine whether player is over `"PressurePlate"` (main) or `"SidePressurePlate"` / `"SidePressurePlate1"` (side).
   - If triggered: Teleports player to `ActiveCheckpointTransform`, halves scale & speed/jump, and updates `ScreenTextComponent` with specific troll message.
3. **Checkpoint Reset & Normalization**:
   - When player reaches a new checkpoint or respawns, `ResetPlayerScaleAndMovement()` restores normal scale (`1.0x`), walk speed, and jump velocity.
